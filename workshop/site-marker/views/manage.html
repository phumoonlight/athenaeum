On-page marker

Puts a dot on every link pointing at a page you've marked — blue for unread, grey for read, gold for a favorite. The dots are display-only; marking is the popup's job. It's per site and starts off everywhere: turn it on for the site you're on from that site's popup. Tabs already open pick the change up without a reload.

Not on for any site yet.

The three settings below are how the dots look wherever they're on, not where they show up.

How big the dots are, in pixels. Bigger is easier to spot on a busy page; smaller keeps them out of the way of dense text. Open tabs resize as you drag.

How solid the dots are. Turn it down to let a dot sit over a page without hiding what's underneath it. Open tabs update as you drag.

Fades the link itself when it points at a page you've marked read, so what's left is what you haven't got to. At 100% the page is left alone. A faded link fades its dot too.
