On-page marker

Links pointing at a page you've marked unread glow blue, and links to pages you've read can be faded, so what stands out is what you haven't got to. Nothing is added to the page, and it's display-only; marking is the popup's job. It's per site and starts off everywhere: turn it on for the site you're on from that site's popup. Tabs already open pick the change up without a reload.

How faded a link is when it points at a page you've marked read. Links to unread pages fade half as far and keep their blue glow — at 40% here, they sit at 70%. At 100% the page is left alone. Open tabs update as you drag.
