On-page marker

Puts a dot on every link pointing at a page you've marked — blue for unread, grey for read, gold for a favorite. The dots are display-only; marking is the popup's job. Applies to tabs you already have open.

At 100% a read link looks like any other and only its dot marks it. Lower it to let things you've finished with recede on a page full of links.
